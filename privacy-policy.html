Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Skin Analyzer (the "App") processes photos to provide skin analysis insights. We are committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining the highest standards of data security.

Key principle: We process your photos in real-time for analysis and immediately delete them. We do not store any facial images, analysis results, or personal information on our servers.

What Data We Do NOT Collect or Store

To be absolutely clear, we do NOT collect, process, or store:

  • ❌ Facial photographs - Images are processed in real-time and immediately deleted
  • ❌ Analysis results - Results are displayed to you but not saved on our servers
  • ❌ Names or email addresses - We don't ask for or store any personal identifiers
  • ❌ Location data - We don't track where you are
  • ❌ Device identifiers - We don't track your device
  • ❌ Usage analytics - We don't monitor how you use the app
  • ❌ User accounts or profiles - No registration or login required
  • ❌ Any personally identifiable information (PII)

Face Data Collection and Use

What We Temporarily Process (Not Store)

When you use Skin Analyzer, you can voluntarily submit facial photographs for skin analysis purposes. We collect only the images you explicitly select for analysis. These images are:

  • Processed in real-time (typically less than 30 seconds)
  • Never saved to disk or database
  • Held only in temporary server memory during processing
  • Immediately and permanently deleted after analysis

How We Use Face Data

Your facial images are used exclusively for:

  • Real-time skin condition analysis (acne, wrinkles, pigmentation, texture, pores, etc.)
  • Generating your personalized skin health score
  • Providing tailored skincare recommendations

Face data is NEVER used for:

  • Facial recognition or identity verification
  • Building databases or user profiles
  • Marketing, advertising, or promotional purposes
  • Training AI models
  • Sharing with advertisers or data brokers
  • Any purpose unrelated to your immediate skin analysis request

Third-Party AI Service Provider

To provide skin analysis, photos are securely transmitted to OpenAI's API for processing. The process:

  • Your photo is sent to OpenAI's API for real-time analysis
  • OpenAI processes the image and returns results
  • Images are immediately deleted from both our servers and OpenAI's systems
  • According to OpenAI's policy, images are NOT used to train AI models

Learn more: OpenAI Enterprise Privacy

We do not share data with any other third parties.

Data Retention

  • Facial photographs: NOT STORED. Processed in real-time and immediately deleted (typically within 30 seconds).
  • Analysis results: NOT STORED. Displayed in real-time only.
  • Personal information: NOT COLLECTED.
  • Usage logs: NOT STORED.

Security

  • Images exist in server memory only during processing (never written to disk)
  • All transmissions are encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • We cannot access historical data because we don't store it

Local Device Storage

If you save analysis results locally on your device:

  • Data stays only on your device
  • Not transmitted to our servers
  • You can delete it anytime

Payments

Payments are handled by Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Your Privacy Rights

Since we don't store any data, there is no data to access, modify, or delete from our servers. Your privacy is protected by design.

Children's Privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at softechabn@gmail.com, and we will take steps to ensure any such information is handled appropriately.

International Data Transfer

Photos are temporarily processed on servers in the United States (OpenAI's infrastructure) during analysis. All data is immediately deleted after processing.
