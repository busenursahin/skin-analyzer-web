Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Skin Analyzer (the "App") processes photos and analysis data to provide skin insights. We aim to keep your data minimal, private, and secure.

Photos & Analysis

Photos you upload for analysis are used to produce the skin analysis results you see. Photos are stored only for the duration of the analysis process and are automatically deleted immediately after processing completes.

Stored Data

Analysis results (for example: skin score, detected concerns) and non-identifying technical logs may be stored anonymously to help improve the service and detect issues. Because the App does not require user login, results are not linked to personal identity unless you explicitly save them to device storage.

Local Storage & History

The app may store quiz results and analysis history locally on your device (via browser localStorage or in-app storage). This stored history remains on your device and is not transmitted off the device by default.

Payments

If you purchase a premium subscription, payments are handled by the platform provider (Apple). We do not receive or store your payment details.

Contact

If you have questions about our privacy practices, email us at softechabn@gmail.com or use the app's support channels.

Changes

Significant changes to this policy will be communicated through the application. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.
